Research Interests
======
* **Nanopore RNA/DNA sequencing** — nonequilibrium transport dynamics of RNA/DNA through protein and solid-state nanopores; coupling of electroosmotic flow and electrophoresis in nanoconfinement.
* **Polymer thermal management materials** — AI-for-Science combined with large-scale molecular dynamics to build structure–property relationships between macromolecular topology and anisotropic thermal conductivity; inverse design via GNNs/PINNs.
* **Polyelectrolyte brushes** — unified theoretical models for brush behavior under ion valency, solvent quality, dipole–dipole interactions, and complex surface architectures (incl. zwitterionic peptide brushes).
* **Liquid–liquid phase separation** — physical predictive models incorporating ion specificity, polarizability, and refractive index; thermodynamic logic and control principles in complex solution systems.
